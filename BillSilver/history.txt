It ran under a custom timesharing system that I wrote sometime in 1976
or 1977 for the MIT Weather Radar Research Project. No OS code or
documentation survives, and I remember only a few things about it. I
can see calls to my OS in the code, for example at sheet 4, line 0206
a system call is made to set process (thread) priority. Priority was a
16-bit word with four 4-bit fields, for timeslot length and priority
level for I/O-bound and compute-bound states, as
dynamically-determined by the OS.

 
The Emacs listing has no date, but my best guess is that it was
written in the summer of 1978. I was a grad student for the 77-78
school year and had been exposed to Emacs under ITS. I returned to
Weather Radar in the summers during grad school.
